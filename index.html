TOOLS & REFERENCES

Quick Editing References

🎞️ FPS

24 FPS is common for cinematic motion. 30 FPS is common for general video. 60 FPS gives smoother motion.

🖥️ Resolution

720p, 1080p, 1440p and 4K describe different video resolutions.

📱 Aspect Ratio

16:9 is common for landscape video. 9:16 is common for Shorts and Reels.

📦 Export

Check resolution, frame rate, codec, bitrate and audio before export.
